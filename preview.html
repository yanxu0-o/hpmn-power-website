About HPMN

Guangdong Huapu Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.

HPMN Power specializes in the R&D and manufacturing of constant voltage switching power supplies for LED applications.

Products are widely used in LED lighting, advertising signage, channel letters, outdoor lighting projects, commercial displays and interior decoration projects. We focus on product quality, reliability and long-term cooperation, providing efficient manufacturing, fast technical support and flexible customization for global distributors, OEM partners and project customers.
